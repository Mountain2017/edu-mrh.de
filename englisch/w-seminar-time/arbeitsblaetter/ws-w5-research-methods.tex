\begin{wsbox}{2}{Five refinery questions}
{\small Questions your paper must answer so that your thesis can stand. Give each one a job, then check it:
\textbf{O}\,= open (no yes/no, no either/or) · \textbf{N}\,= necessary (without it: a gap) · \textbf{A}\,= answerable (sources or own data).}\par\vspace{1mm}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{20mm} X *{3}{>{\centering\arraybackslash}p{5mm}}@{}}
\arrayrulecolor{rule}\toprule
\textbf{job} & \textbf{my refinery question} & \textbf{O} & \textbf{N} & \textbf{A}\\\midrule
\rule[-9mm]{0pt}{19mm}\job{dphys}{1 Define}\newline{\footnotesize\textcolor{muted}{→ ch.\,2}} & \lsgtext{How do psychologists define “perceived duration”, and how does it differ from clock time?} & \hk & \hk & \hk\\\midrule
\rule[-9mm]{0pt}{19mm}\job{dlang}{2 Explain}\newline{\footnotesize\textcolor{muted}{→ ch.\,2}} & \lsgtext{Which models explain why the same duration can feel shorter or longer – and what role do attention and arousal play?} & \hk & \hk & \hk\\\midrule
\rule[-9mm]{0pt}{19mm}\job{dlit}{3 Context}\newline{\footnotesize\textcolor{muted}{→ ch.\,1}} & \lsgtext{In which everyday situations does waiting matter, and how do shops or hospitals try to make it feel shorter?} & \hk & \hk & \hk\\\midrule
\rule[-9mm]{0pt}{19mm}\job{dcs}{4 Test}\newline{\footnotesize\textcolor{muted}{→ ch.\,3 + 4}} & \lsgtext{How large is the difference between the fast-music and the slow-music group in my experiment, and how reliable is it?} & \hk & \hk & \hk\\\midrule
\rule[-9mm]{0pt}{19mm}\job{dpsy}{5 Evaluate}\newline{\footnotesize\textcolor{muted}{→ ch.\,5 + 6}} & \lsgtext{To what extent can the results of one school experiment be generalised, and which other factors could explain them?} & \hk & \hk & \hk\\\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{wsbox}
