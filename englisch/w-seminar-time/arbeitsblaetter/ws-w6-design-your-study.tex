{Before you collect a single number: find the kind of data that can really answer your question – and talk your plan through.}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------ 1
\begin{wsbox}{1}{My question \enspace{\normalfont\small\textcolor{muted}{from W4 – research question and working thesis / hypothesis}}}
\begin{lueckentext}
\kl{Question:}\enspace\lsgrest{How does the tempo of background music change how long a wait feels?}
\kl{Thesis:}\enspace\lsgrest{Students who wait with fast music estimate a 3-minute wait as shorter than with slow music.}
\end{lueckentext}
\end{wsbox}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------ 2
\begin{wsbox}{2}{Which quality criterion is at risk?}
{\small \textbf{O}bjectivity: same result whoever runs or scores it · \textbf{R}eliability: same result again, little noise · \textbf{V}alidity: measures what you claim. Tick the criterion that is hit first.}\par\vspace{0.8mm}

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}X *{3}{>{\centering\arraybackslash}p{6mm}}@{}}
\arrayrulecolor{rule}\toprule
 & \textbf{O} & \textbf{R} & \textbf{V}\\\midrule
a) You mark students’ open answers yourself – without a marking key. & \hkx & \hk & \hk\\\midrule
b) One single question (“Are you stressed?”) is your whole measure of stress. & \hk & \hkx & \hk\\\midrule
c) A speed-reading task is used to show how well students \emph{understand} a novel. & \hk & \hk & \hkx\\\midrule
d) Group A waits before lunch, group B after lunch. & \hk & \hk & \hkx\\\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{wsbox}

\begin{wsbox}{5}{My design \enspace{\normalfont\small\textcolor{muted}{decide – you can still change it after the pilot}}}
{\small\cb survey\quad \cb test\quad \cbx experiment\quad \cb coding texts\quad \cb existing data\quad \cb mixed: \luecke[40mm]}\par\vspace{0.5mm}
\begin{lueckentext}
\kl{Idea $\rightarrow$ rule (operationalisation):}\enspace\lsgrest{“time flies” $\rightarrow$ estimate of a 3-min wait in minutes}
\kl{Variables / items / categories:}\enspace\lsgrest{IV tempo (fast / slow), DV estimate; same room, volume}
